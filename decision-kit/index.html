1. Overview

Decision Kit is a free offline decision-making tool developed by A2H Software. It provides four randomness tools to help you break decision paralysis:

🎡 Spin Wheel 🪙 Coin Toss 🎲 Dice Roller 🃏 Card Picker

This Privacy Policy describes what information the app handles, how it is used, and your rights as a user. By installing or using Decision Kit, you acknowledge this policy.

📋

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Decision Kit does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or unique hardware identifiers
  • Location data (GPS, IP address, or any geolocation)
  • Usage analytics, session data, or behavioural tracking
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs sent to any server
  • Content you enter into the app (your options, questions, labels)
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or contact data — we request none of these permissions
In short: There is no server. There is no database. There is no account. Nothing you type or tap ever leaves your device.
💾

3. Local Data Storage

Decision Kit saves your preferences and options locally on your device using Android's standard local storage (AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your phone and is not accessible to A2H Software or any third party.

The following data is stored locally so your setup persists between app sessions:

Storage Key What it saves
settings-store Sound effects toggle (on/off), haptic feedback toggle (on/off)
wheel-store Your spin wheel question and the list of options you've added
coin-store Custom labels for "Heads" and "Tails" faces
dice-game-store Your dice roller question and up to 6 options
card-store Your card picker question and up to 52 options

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by using the "Clear All" button within each tool, or by uninstalling the app.

🔑

4. Device Permissions

Decision Kit requests only one device capability:

  • Vibration — used solely to provide haptic feedback (tactile pulses) when you interact with the tools. This is entirely optional; you can disable it in the app's Settings screen.

Decision Kit does not request or use:

  • Camera or microphone access
  • Location or GPS permissions
  • Contacts, calendar, or files
  • Internet / network access (no INTERNET permission declared on Android)
  • Push notifications
  • Biometric authentication
🔗

5. Third-Party Services

Decision Kit is built with Expo and React Native — open-source frameworks. No third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Mixpanel, Crashlytics) are integrated into this app.

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google may collect certain data as part of the Play Store platform independently of this app (e.g. crash reports you opt into via device settings). This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data to share.

👧

6. Children's Privacy

Decision Kit does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app operates entirely offline and stores no user data on any server, it poses no privacy risk to children. The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent regulations.

🛡️

7. Data Security

Because Decision Kit stores all data locally on your device and transmits nothing over any network, the security of your data depends on your device's own security (screen lock, device encryption, etc.). We do not maintain any servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure that could be compromised.
